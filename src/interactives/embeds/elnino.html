Marine Science IGCSE · 2.6 Upwelling & El Niño

Normal year vs El Niño

A slice across the Pacific — Australia and Asia on the left, South America on the right. Flip between a normal year and an El Niño and watch the winds, the warm water and the upwelling change.

A normal year: strong east→west trade winds pile warm surface water in the west and let cold, nutrient-rich water upwell in the east.

West Australia & Asia
Rising warm, moist air brings the region its normal rainfall.
East South America / eastern Pacific
Cold upwelling brings nutrients → plankton → a large fish catch; drier coast.
!
Common traps: El Niño is a Pacific event, not Atlantic; the trade winds weaken or reverse (they don't strengthen); and eastern-Pacific upwelling falls (it doesn't rise).
