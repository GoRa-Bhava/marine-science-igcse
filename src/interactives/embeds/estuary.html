Marine Science IGCSE · 5.6 & 2.2 Estuaries

An estuary through the tide

Slide from low tide to high tide and watch three things change together. One of them moves the opposite way to most people's first guess.

TideLow tide
Salinitylow (nearly fresh)
Temperature stabilityvariable
Dissolved oxygenlower

At low tide the river dominates: the water is nearly fresh, shallow and follows the air, so it warms and cools quickly and holds little oxygen.

!
The trap: salinity is not highest at low tide, even though the shallow water evaporates. It is highest at high tide, when salty sea water pushes in. Watch the salinity bar rise as you move to high tide.
