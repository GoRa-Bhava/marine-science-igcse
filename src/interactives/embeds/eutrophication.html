Where the oxygen goes

Step through the process. Keep your eye on the dissolved-oxygen graph — it rises before it crashes, and that rise is the trap most answers fall into.

0Balanced water

Oxygen from the algae's photosynthesis and from the air balances what the animals use up. Dissolved oxygen sits at a healthy level.

!
The trap: "more algae → more oxygen." The living bloom does add oxygen for a while — but once it dies, decomposers use up far more than the algae ever produced. Net oxygen falls.
