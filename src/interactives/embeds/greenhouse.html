Quick self-check

Do greenhouse gases work by blocking incoming sunlight?

Advance the process, then choose.

Greenhouse gases let the Sun's rays in freely — they don't block incoming sunlight. What they trap is the heat radiated back from the surface, and more gas traps more of it, so the temperature climbs.

The rays show a qualitative comparison, not measured amounts. Returning arrows represent heat absorbed and re-emitted towards the surface.
