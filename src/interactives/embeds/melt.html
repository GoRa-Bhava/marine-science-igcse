The exam trap

Where was the ice before it melted?

Floating sea ice → no change

Floating ice displaces its own weight of water. When it melts, the meltwater exactly fills the space the submerged ice took up, so the water level is unchanged.

Land ice → sea level rises

Ice resting on land is not yet in the sea. When it melts, the water runs off the land into the sea and adds to it, so sea level rises.

Fresh meltwater also lowers the sea’s salinity and density.

Thermal expansion: a warming ocean expands, raising sea level a second way.

In this comparison, melting land ice — such as glaciers and ice sheets in Greenland and Antarctica — raises sea level. Floating Arctic pack ice does not. Simplified teaching model; not to scale.
